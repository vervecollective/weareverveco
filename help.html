Welcome

This is the Verve Collective platform. Everything you need lives in the menu on the left.

The first thing to do

  1. Open Settings → Profile and add your name, photo, and phone. Your name is what everyone else sees next to your work.
  2. Go to Settings → Security and set a password you will remember. If you arrived from an invite email, you may not have one yet.
  3. Come back here and read the section for your role in the menu on the left.
You will never need to type a web address. Everything is in the left menu. If you cannot find something, it is either not built yet or not available to your role — ask rather than hunting for it.

Running the business

This is the owner view. You see everything.

Your daily loop

  1. Home — the seven-step client journey. Click any step to open the tool for it.
  2. Call Console — open this before any call. Pick the stage you are in and the script follows the conversation.
  3. Engagements — build the scope, price it, send the invoice.
  4. People — invite contractors and account owners, set their roles.

Adding a new client

  1. Open Engagements and click + New engagement.
  2. Fill in the business, contact name, and email. The email matters — it is where the invoice goes.
  3. Use Quick add to drop in services from the catalogue, or add blank lines and type your own.
  4. Put the contractor cost in its own column. The margin panel shows what you actually keep.
  5. Click Save, then Create & send invoice.
Contract before invoice, always. Your own process says the agreement is signed before money is requested and before any work starts. The system will let you skip it — do not.

Who found the client matters

The Lead source dropdown sets the split with an account owner. Verve-sourced is 40/60, owner-sourced is 20/80, own-brand is 10/90. Whatever it is when you save is locked to that engagement forever — changing your defaults later never rewrites old deals.

For account owners

You run your own client accounts inside Verve's system.

What you can see

Only your own clients and engagements. Other account owners' accounts are invisible to you, and theirs to you. That is enforced by the database, not just hidden.

Why the difference? Finding a client is the most expensive thing an agency does. When Verve hands you a signed client, that cost is already paid. When you find your own, you carried it — so you keep more.

For contractors

You shoot, edit, or create. Here is everything you need.

What you will see

Your menu is short on purpose — Home, My Jobs, and Settings. You will never see client pricing, other contractors' rates, or what Verve makes on a job. That is deliberate and it is enforced by the system, not by trust.

Getting a job

  1. You get an email when a job is offered to you.
  2. Open My Jobs. You will see the date, location, what you are being asked to deliver, and exactly what you are being paid.
  3. Accept or decline. Declining is fine and costs you nothing — say so early rather than late.
  4. Once accepted, the date is yours and the details stay on that page.

Before your first job

  1. Sign the contractor agreement and IP assignment we send you. Without these we legally cannot use your footage for the client, so nothing can start until they are done.
  2. Send us a W-9. You are a 1099 contractor, so we report what we pay you at year end.
  3. Fill in your craft, day rate, and gear in Settings so you get offered the right work.

Getting paid

You invoice us per deliverable, not per hour. We pay you on our terms whether or not the client has paid us yet — that timing is our problem, not yours.

For clients

Everything about your project, in one place.

Your project page

Click My Project in the menu. You will see:

  1. Where things stand — a progress bar from first enquiry to complete, with a plain sentence explaining what the current stage means.
  2. Timeline — the dates we are working to. Anything marked "Needs you" in amber is waiting on you, and it is the fastest way to unblock your own project.
  3. Billing — what is owed and what is paid, with a Pay button if there is a balance.
  4. Ask us something — type a question and it goes straight to your account lead. Your history and our answers stay on the page.
About ad spend. If we run ads for you, your card is on Google and Meta directly. You see every dollar in real time, in those platforms. Our invoice only ever shows what you pay us to manage it — never blended together.

Common questions

I signed in but the page looks empty.

That usually means there is nothing there yet rather than something broken. Empty pages explain what to do next — read the message in the middle.

Why can I not see something a colleague can?

The menu changes by role. Contractors see jobs, clients see their project, account owners see their own accounts, and the owner sees everything. It is not hidden from you by accident.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen. It emails you a reset link. Nobody at Verve can see or set your password for you.

Do I need to remember any web addresses?

No. Everything is in the left menu. If a page is not there, you do not have access to it or it is not built yet.

Something is broken. What do I do?

Tell Trae exactly which page you were on and what you clicked. "It is broken" takes three messages to diagnose; "the Save button on Engagements did nothing" takes one.
