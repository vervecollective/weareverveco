Welcome

Everything you need is in the menu on the left.

Do these three things first

  1. Open Settings → Profile. Add your name and photo — that is what everyone else sees next to your work.
  2. While you are there, set your availability: how many hours a week you can take, and which days and times. You set it, not us. It is what we check before offering you work.
  3. Read the section for your role in the menu on the left. It is short.
You never need to type a web address. Everything is in the left menu. On a phone it is behind the button at the top left. If a page is not listed, it is not available to your role.
Clicking something opens it in a panel, not a new page. You keep your place. Every panel has Open fully if you want the whole screen, and Esc closes it.

Running the business

You see everything.

A normal day

  1. Open Home. Work the "Needs you" list top down — it is ordered by what costs most if it waits, not by date.
  2. Before any call, open the Call Console and pick the stage you are in.
  3. After a discovery call, build the scope in Engagements, then send a proposal from the same page.
  4. Check Capacity before saying yes to anything new.

Taking a new client

  1. Engagements → + New engagement. Business, contact, email. Pick or create an organization if they have several locations.
  2. Add line items. Put the contractor cost in its own column — the margin panel then shows what you actually keep.
  3. Set hours per week. That is what Capacity reads.
  4. Write the proposal in the Proposals section and send it.
  5. When they agree: contract from Documents, then the deposit invoice.
Contract before invoice, invoice before work. The system will let you skip it. Do not.

Who found the client changes the split

The Lead source field sets it: Verve-sourced 40/60, owner-sourced 20/80, own-brand 10/90. It freezes when you save, so changing your defaults later never rewrites old deals.

For account owners

You run your own client accounts inside Verve's system.

What you can see

Only your own clients and engagements. Other account owners' work is invisible to you, and yours to them. That is enforced by the database, not by hiding menu items.

Why the difference? Finding a client is the most expensive thing an agency does. When Verve hands you a signed client that cost is already paid. When you find your own, you carried it, so you keep more.

Keep Capacity honest

Add yourself to an engagement's account team with realistic weekly hours. Capacity is only as good as what is in it, and it is what decides whether we say yes to the next project.

For contractors

Short menu on purpose. You will not see client pricing, other contractors' rates, or what Verve makes. That is enforced by the system, not by trust.

Before your first job

  1. Sign the contractor agreement and the confidentiality and IP assignment we email you. Without the IP assignment we legally cannot license your footage to the client, so nothing can start.
  2. Fill in Pay → Your details — legal name, address, how you want to be paid, and the last four digits of your tax ID. We need it for your 1099.
  3. Set your availability in Settings so you are offered work that fits.
Never send full bank details or a full tax ID through the site. Give us the method and we will confirm the account with you directly.

Getting a job

  1. You get an email. The button opens the job even if you are signed out.
  2. Jobs shows the call sheet: every day and call time, location, what to bring, what we provide, who to find on site.
  3. The big number is everything you receive — fee plus mileage plus per diem. Your base fee is underneath it.
  4. Accept or decline. Declining is completely fine and costs you nothing. Telling us early is worth more than saying yes reluctantly.

Getting paid

  1. After the work, go to Pay → Invoice us and pick the job. The amount fills in from what was agreed.
  2. Add any expenses and your own invoice number if you use one.
  3. We approve it and pay within 15 days.
You are paid on our schedule, not the client's. If a client pays us late that is our problem, not yours.

For clients

Everything about your project in one place.

My Project

  1. Where things stand — a progress bar with a plain sentence explaining the current stage.
  2. Timeline — our dates. Anything marked Needs you in amber is waiting on you, and it is the fastest way to unblock your own project.
  3. What's happened — updates as work moves, without you having to ask.
  4. Billing — what is paid and what is owed, with a pay button if there is a balance.
  5. Ask us something — goes straight to your account lead, and the history stays on the page.
About ad spend. If we run ads for you, your card is on Google and Meta directly. You see every dollar in real time in those platforms. Our invoice only ever shows what you pay us to manage it — never blended together.

Common questions

A page looks empty.

Usually there is nothing there yet rather than something broken. Empty pages explain what belongs there — read the message in the middle. On the Board, check whether a filter is hiding things; the line under the filters says how many are hidden.

Why can I not see what a colleague sees?

The menu changes by role. Contractors see their work, clients see their project, account owners see their own accounts, the owner sees everything. It is deliberate.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen. Nobody at Verve can see or set it for you.

Can I send someone a link to one task?

Yes. Open it on the Board and press Copy link. It opens that exact task for them, switching account automatically.

Something is broken.

Tell Trae which page you were on and what you clicked. "The Save button on Engagements did nothing" takes one message; "it is broken" takes three.
