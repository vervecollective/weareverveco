Privacy Policy

Verve Collective LLC · Columbia, Missouri

Verve Collective LLC ("Verve Collective," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through weareverveco.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us directly — your name, email, business name, and anything you tell us about your ad spend or situation, submitted through our contact form or our short intake form.
  • Scheduling information if you book a discovery call through our Google Calendar booking link.
  • Basic technical and usage data collected automatically through our website platform (for example, pages visited), and — only if you accept optional cookies — analytics data that helps us understand how the site is used.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a discovery call.
  • To prepare proposals, contracts, and invoices if you become a client.
  • To send occasional updates about our services, with an easy way to opt out at any time.
  • To understand and improve how our website works, if you've opted in to optional cookies.

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. We do not share it with anyone outside the tools listed below, except as required by law.

Cookies

Our site uses necessary cookies that keep the site functioning (for example, remembering your cookie preference) — these are always on and can't be turned off, since the site wouldn't work correctly without them.

If we add optional cookies in the future (for example, analytics to see which pages are useful), they will only run after you click "Accept all" on the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer of this site.

Tools that may process data on our behalf

  • Netlify — hosts this website and processes contact form submissions. Netlify's privacy policy
  • Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) — our business email and call scheduling run on Google Workspace. Google's privacy policy
  • HubSpot — our internal CRM, used to track client and prospect relationships. HubSpot's privacy policy

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it, at any time — just email us and we'll take care of it. You can also opt out of any marketing email using the unsubscribe link in that email, or by asking us directly.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Missouri.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or services change. The current version is always posted at this address.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at contact@weareverveco.com.
